About me

How a show with me goes

Mind reading is the center of it. A guest thinks of a name or a choice, I try to say what it is, and the people around them get to watch that happen. There's some psychology in how I get there, and a little magic when I want the moment in someone's hands.

Stage show

Book this when your guests will be seated and facing the same way. I do mind reading from the stage, and I bring a few people into it with me. Everyone else watches, which is a big part of why it works.

Close-Up Photo
Photo

Strolling and close-up

This fits a cocktail hour or a reception, when people are standing and talking. I walk up to a small group, work with them, and move on. If you'd rather have everyone gathered in one place, I can do a close-up set instead, right in their hands.
